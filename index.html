About

I like the moment a pipeline goes from "seventeen different systems reporting seventeen different truths" to one number everyone trusts. That's what I spent a year and a half doing at IDFC First Bank, migrating a real-time Account Aggregator platform to AWS and building the fraud-detection layer on top of it.

Before that, at Capgemini, I worked on the less glamorous, higher-stakes job of migrating a client's Oracle systems into Microsoft Fabric, the kind of project where "boring and reliable" is the highest compliment. Somewhere in between, I got curious about what happens after the pipeline runs, which is what sent me back to Galway for an MSc in Data Analytics.
